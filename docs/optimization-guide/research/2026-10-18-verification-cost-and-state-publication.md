# Verification cost and accepted-state publication

Read2026-10-18 during the observer-OFF combined timing experiment. Source review only; no downloaded runtime, benchmark, GPU experiment or imported paper speedup.

[**GDN Tree-Scan, v1,2026-09-20**](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23900v1), sections IV–VIII: the verifier carries branch-local recurrent state and publishes only the accepted chain, with matching convolution-state remapping. The native recurrence's rounding and reduction boundaries remain relevant even when attention ancestry is correct. The reported Qwen3.6-27B-FP8 B1 result has different token-weighted decode and equal-request latency gains; equivalence is scoped to a measured native probability-rescore floor. It is not a bitwise proof for our NVFP4 target, or an M5/GLM result. Transfer the explicit parent-state and accepted-publication contracts, not its Triton geometry or numerical tolerance.

[**EVICT, v2,2026-09-05**](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00342v2), sections2.2–3.3 and4.3: a larger draft tree can increase the union of routed MoE experts and verification traffic. The method selects a valid tree prefix using estimated accepted length divided by profiled full speculative-step cost, with multiple captured verification lengths. Maximizing accepted tokens alone is insufficient. For GLM this suggests measuring actual expert unions and complete iteration cost before selecting block width. Its CUDA/SGLang results do not predict this model's mixed-quantization, KDA/DSA/mHC implementation, and adaptive pruning cannot skip the exact target arithmetic for a retained token.

[**Mirror-SD, Apple Research,2025-12**](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/mirror): complementary draft/target correction pipelines and speculative streaming reduce serial draft delay through heterogeneous execution. This is an older architectural reference, not newly published October work. Its reported server-model results do not establish that our Metal target plus a CPU/ANE drafter will overlap profitably. Actual dependencies, draft compatibility and shared-memory traffic must be measured first.

Our next concrete boundary remains the [native verifier bridge](../../../bench/reviews/2026-10-18-exact-verifier-bridge-review.md): independently continue the same committed state with true C1 decode and the existing short verification expression; retain original-dtype full logits and cache arrays at the first mismatch. T1 prefill is not an acceptable replacement oracle. Forward equality, accepted-prefix rollback, subsequent continuation, and complete HTTP latency are separate questions. A useful block must reduce total time per committed token, including drafting and state work; none of these papers waives that measurement.
